What Is This Channel?

An Introductory Statement

The digital landspace, especially on site such as YouTube, has become incredibly populated and overcrowded as the world has continued to rely more on technology. This had made it incredibly hard to stand out and be unique, but I wanted to try nonetheless

I originally created this channel and edited videos of me playing games with friends, games like Fortnite or R.E.P.O, popular games that viewers have seen a million times over. There are a limitless amount of channels doing exactly that, just playing games with friends, and some do find success in such videos, but most fall into obscurity. Once I realized that, I wanted to change things up and try to stand out just a little more. So now I upload videos that I would call "narrated playthroughs". Essentially, I will play through a game I have never played before, recording my thoughts as I go along. Once I have played through the game, I will create a script which I will use to narrate over my playthrough, making sure it syncs to whatever gameplay is currently being shown.

Thumbnails

All thumbnails are created by me, for my channel. Nowadays, there has been an upsurge in AI generated thumbnails, or even fully AI generated channels. I want to assure people that I would not use AI for any creative process on my channel. Though my art and editing still have a long way to go, it makes me proud to create pieces of art and videos for people to enjoy.

Video Timeline

Ideally, I would like to get a video out every month or so. Unfortunately, I have not been able to keep that schedule thus far and it has been more like every 4 months. The process behind each video is quite long, and I am very bad at just sitting down and starting a project without being told by another person. Once I get into a routine and learn to set timelines and reminders, I believe the workflow will go by much smoother and videos will come out faster without compromising quality.

As my editing skills continue to grow, I hope to put out even higher quality videos that people enjoy. I make each video in a way that I would enjoy watching it myself, so I can come back to each video and be taken back to when I was working on it. My philosophy is, if I enjoy the video, the viewers will too. In addition, as my channel continues to grow I would like to make a second channel at some point, where I post videos that I had been making before my switch in style, such as just playing with friends. If people come to enjoy my personality in my main channel videos, then people would enjoy the videos with my friends. Of course, that's for a while down the line, but I like to think the future of this channel is looking positive.
